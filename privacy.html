Privacy Policy

Nest Notification Bridge
Effective October 18, 2026

Overview

Nest Notification Bridge is a private, single-user, non-commercial project operated for the Google account owner's own Google Nest devices. It is not a public service and does not offer accounts to other people.

Information accessed

When the account owner authorizes the project through Google, the project may access information made available by the Google Smart Device Management API for the homes and devices the owner selects. That information can include:

  • Home, room, device names, device types, status, and device traits;
  • Camera event details and timestamps;
  • Camera snapshots, images, clips, or live-stream connection information, when the owner requests or configures a feature that needs them; and
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens used to maintain the authorized connection.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate the bridge for the account owner: identifying authorized devices, reporting device or camera events, returning requested snapshots or streams, and maintaining the connection with Google's API. Information is not used for advertising, profiling, credit or lending decisions, or sale to third parties.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's private home server or other owner-controlled equipment and are protected with restrictive file permissions. Device information is processed on owner-controlled equipment for the purpose requested by the owner. Temporary stream URLs are not intentionally published or retained after use.

Sharing

Information is not sold or shared with third parties for their own use. Communication occurs between the bridge and Google's services as necessary to perform the authorized API requests. Information may be disclosed only if required by law.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and project data are retained only while the owner keeps the bridge connected or as needed to operate a feature the owner configured. The owner can stop future access at any time by revoking Nest Notification Bridge in the Google Account's third-party access settings. Revoking access invalidates the authorization; locally stored tokens and project data can then be deleted from the owner's equipment.

Google API use

Nest Notification Bridge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email address shown for Nest Notification Bridge on Google's OAuth consent screen, or submitted through this project's GitHub repository.
